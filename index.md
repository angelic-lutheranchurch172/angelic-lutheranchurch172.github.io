## 🛠️ Features

Sketchetc includes several tools to help your computer run better.

### Clipboard History
Computers often forget what you copied five minutes ago. This tool saves your text and images. You can view your history at any time and paste items back into your workflow.

### Window Management
Manage your workspace with ease. The software includes tools to snap windows to the sides of your screen. This creates a clean layout and helps you focus on your active task.

### Tamper-proof Journal
Write your thoughts in a private space. The journal uses encryption to protect your notes from unwanted eyes. Only you hold the key to your files.

### System Monitor
Keep track of how your computer functions. The system monitor shows how much power your apps use. It highlights performance issues before they cause slow speeds.

## ⚙️ How to use the software

Once you install the software, an icon appears in your system tray. This area sits near your clock at the bottom right of the screen. 

Right-click the icon to see a menu of options. You can toggle the window manager, open your clipboard, or launch the journal from this list. 

Select Preferences if you wish to change the look of the software. You can choose different themes that match the Vice City style. These settings apply immediately to your desktop.

## 🔒 Security and Privacy

Your data remains on your machine. The software does not send your files or clipboard history to third-party servers. All encryption happens locally. If you delete a journal entry, the software removes the data from your drive permanently.
